## 🌍 Overview  

**SafeDost.AI** is an AI-powered safety assistant designed to provide **real-time guidance, verified information, and actionable support** in unsafe situations.

> 🚀 A step towards **accessible, intelligent, and always-available safety support**

---

## ✨ Core Features  

### ⚡ Real-Time Guidance  
- Instant help during critical situations  
- Context-aware responses  

### 🔐 Verified Knowledge  
- Trained on safety laws, helplines, and awareness guides  
- Reliable and trustworthy outputs  

### 🧠 AI Intelligence  
- Powered by LLMs + Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)  
- Understands natural language queries
